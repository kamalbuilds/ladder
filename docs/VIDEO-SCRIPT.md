# Ladder — demo video script

Target: under 3 minutes. Read at ~150 words per minute.
One beat = one narration line + one clip, clip padded to exactly the beat length.

Total: 9 beats, 397 words, 2:39.

## Beat 1 — Open (10s, 25 words)
**Footage:** `01-gate.mp4` — the live site at focused-mink-234.convex.site, gate on screen.

> This is Ladder. When a company stops replying to you, Ladder finds who actually
> regulates them, tracks the legal deadline, and escalates the day it runs out.

---

## Beat 2 — The problem (30s, 75 words)
**Footage:** `02-problem.mp4` — the real Reddit threads we sourced, scrolling.

> A passenger had five hundred and twenty pounds of flight compensation confirmed in
> writing. He sent his bank details as instructed, then got three months of silence.
> The problem was never the argument. The published contact address was not the desk
> that pays, and nobody told him. Every complaint dies the same two ways. You do not
> know who has authority over them, and the clock runs out while you find out.

---

## Beat 3 — Create a case (25s, 62 words)
**Footage:** `03-create.mp4` — typing the case, clicking Map the ladder.

> So you tell Ladder who has gone quiet. A letting agent, a repair ignored for six
> weeks. Firecrawl reads their own complaints page and then goes looking for whoever
> regulates them. Nothing here is a lookup table. Every ladder is crawled at the moment
> you ask.

---

## Beat 4 — The ladder, with sources (30s, 75 words)
**Footage:** `04-ladder.mp4` — the three rungs, clocks counting, clicking a source link
through to the real Foxtons page.

> And here is the thing people get wrong. This agent answers to The Property Ombudsman.
> Ladder did not guess that, it read it, and it will show you the sentence and the page
> it came from. Fifteen working days for their final response. Twelve months to refer it
> onward. Those are their published numbers, not ours. Click any of them and you land on
> the real page.

---

## Beat 5 — The deadline runs out (25s, 62 words)
**Footage:** `05-escalate.mp4` — Wind the clock, rung goes red, escalation email appears.

> Real windows are weeks long, so this button winds one past its deadline. Everything
> after it is real. The cron catches the expired clock, OpenAI drafts the escalation
> citing the window that passed, and AgentMail sends it from this case's own inbox.

## Beat 6 — Reading the replies (25s, 62 words)
